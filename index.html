How does it work?

The app tries street junctions as places to split off. It picks the route with the most walking together that fits both time limits, and skips routes that visit the same junction twice.

Times assume a steady 4.9 km/h. They don’t include crossing delays, opening hours or closures. The map covers the area around UCL, and doesn’t check whether a route is step-free.
